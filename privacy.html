Privacy Policy

Currency Calculator · Last updated 2 September 2026

Currency Calculator (the “app”) is a simple calculator with a currency‑conversion feature. Your privacy matters, and the app is built to need as little data as possible. The app does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

Information we collect

None We do not ask you to sign in, and we do not collect names, emails, contacts, location, or any personal or device identifiers. There are no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind.

Data stored on your device

To make the app convenient and usable offline, a few small settings are saved locally in your browser’s storage on your device only:

  • Your selected “from” and “to” currencies and last‑used mode.
  • The most recently downloaded exchange rates (so the app works without a connection).

This information never leaves your device and is not accessible to us. You can clear it at any time by clearing the app’s site data or uninstalling the app.

Exchange‑rate requests

To show up‑to‑date rates, the app fetches publicly available exchange‑rate data from third‑party providers:

  • open.er-api.com (ExchangeRate‑API)
  • frankfurter.dev

When the app requests rates, those providers receive the standard technical information that any website receives with a request (such as your device’s IP address). We do not send them any information about you, and no currency amounts you type are transmitted — all calculations and conversions happen locally on your device. Please refer to each provider’s own policy for how they handle requests.

Children’s privacy

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address with a new “last updated” date.
